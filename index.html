Waitlist and 4-lesson trial process

New students do not see the regular schedule. They join the waitlist first. When a student drops, the open weekly time can be offered as a 4-lesson trial.

3

Trial block

The family takes that same weekly time for 4 lessons.

4

Continue or release

You decide whether the student keeps the time or the slot reopens.
